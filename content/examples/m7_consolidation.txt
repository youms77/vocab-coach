For my seminar paper, I want to shed light on the effect of schooling on wages. First, I run a regression of wages on the years of schooling. This is not enough, because people choose how long they study, so I use the distance to college as an instrumental variable. With this identification strategy, I can tease out the causal effect from the role of ability. I also take into account the age and the gender of each worker. The result is interesting, but our noisy estimate are still too imprecise to be sure.
