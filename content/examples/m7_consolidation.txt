For my seminar paper, I want to shed light on the effect of schooling on wages. To do this, I want to run a regression of wages on the years of schooling. This is not enough, because people choose how long they study, so I plan to use the distance to college as an instrumental variable. With this identification strategy, I can tease out the causal effect. I also need to take into account the age and the gender of each worker. At the end, I noisy estimate the effect of schooling with the survey data.
